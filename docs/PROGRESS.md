# stimjimAWG progress index

One line per phase, newest first. Full handoff entries — decisions, rationale, open
questions, next entry point — live in `docs/progress/<NNN>-<topic>.md` (project convention,
CLAUDE.md). To pick up work read this index, then at most the newest one or two entries.

- 015 2026-09-05 ui-results-clock — the box now runs headless and says so (the OLED library never probes the bus, so an unplugged panel used to cost up to 340 ms of blocked `loop()` per frame and starve the MDATA drain; `UiMenu` probes it itself and gates only the frame push, so `SCREEN` still works with nothing connected), Btn1/Btn2 fire the two trigger routes again through a loop()-context `Triggers::fireRoute` with arm/re-arm debounce (one press is one train whatever the contact does) while Btn0 walks a flat page list — STATUS, up to four RESULT pages of the last train's per-point V, I and load resistance per channel, SYSTEM — also reachable by `PAGE`; resistance is printed to the precision the two standard errors support (floored at 1 % for the uncharacterised ADC gain, capped at three significant figures, with `open` and `--` where a number would lie) and readings at the driver's 9 V ceiling or the pump's 3 mA limit are marked `*`; a new `Clock` module reads and sets the RTC on both MCU families and reports whether its epoch is a compile time, a battery or a host, with `CLK?` pairing it with the microsecond timebase and the log carrying `# clock:` anchors instead of new columns; and the per-row number formatting went fixed point, taking `_dtoa_r` out of the row path, with `BENCHFMT` added to measure what is left — four configurations and five host suites clean, but **nothing run on silicon**, so `BENCHFMT` and `timing.md` §6 still carry a placeholder (progress/015-ui-results-clock.md)
- 014 2026-09-04 prearm — the whole arm moved out of the trigger ISR: two `Player` buffers per engine, `loop()` prepares the one the next `TRIG` route would fire, and the edge places `t0`, rebases the envelope, attaches the plan, swaps the pointer and programs the timer, so what is left after an edge is two `cycles64()` reads and a few dozen instructions on any train shape; the stage look-ahead became structural (derive the next stage, and one more only behind a 0-duration jump) and a short-stage `WARN` now catches stage boundaries closer than one latch costs at definition time — four builds and four host suites clean, `CAL STARTLAT` deliberately left at 35 until `bench_arm.py` re-measures it on silicon (progress/014-prearm.md)
- 013 2026-09-03 lazy-stages — the per-stage derivation moved out of the arm and into the player: the arm derives stage 0 and `deriveAhead` derives stage i+1 in the idle gap before a latch, so a ten-stage `L` arm fell 28.7 → 15.0 µs and a ten-stage `S` 17.5 → 13.3 µs, every stress train (12 µs `S` boundaries, the `dt` floor, a 0-duration jump chain) keeps every deadline, and `CAL STARTLAT` drops 45 → 35 µs — verified by bisection under real trigger edges, because a `T` start is never charged for the arm (progress/013-lazy-stages.md)
- 012 2026-09-03 plan-out-of-arm — the measurement plan compiled and its accumulators cleared in `loop()` instead of the arm, on the back of two plan buffers per engine: in-train measurement now costs the arm nothing (a ten-point train fell 54.3 → 17.5 µs), the worst arm of any shape is a ten-stage `L` train's 28.7 µs, and `CAL STARTLAT` drops 60 → 45 µs — the first budget this project has lowered (progress/012-plan-out-of-arm.md)
- 011 2026-09-03 arm-measured — the arm measured on silicon: 8.45 µs floor, 0.89 µs per `S` stage, 1.93 µs per `L` stage, 0.55 µs per measurement point and 3.1 µs more per point on the first arm after an edit, which makes the worst case 54.3 µs and keeps `CAL STARTLAT` at 60; `SJ_CODE_IN_RAM` is worth 13–17 % and now covers `rampStageInit`/`rampStep`; the 8 µs goal is retired against an 8.45 µs floor (progress/011-arm-measured.md)
- 010 2026-09-03 arm-cost — the start latency accounted for term by term and most of the arm moved out of it: plan compiled once per definition, only the used plan zeroed, sine constants derived at parse time, exact 32-bit ramp divisions, hot functions in RAM (`SJ_CODE_IN_RAM`), consecutive 0-duration `L` stages refused; `docs/timing.md` is new, predictions await `bench_arm.py` on silicon (progress/010-arm-cost.md)
- 009 2026-09-03 measured-budgets — `STARTLAT` and `SETTLE` measured on silicon and both corrected (20 → 60 µs, 4 → 9 µs), `BENCHARM` and `BENCHSETTLE` added, latch contention quantified, timing and parametrization figures, README as a quick start, EEPROM v6 (progress/009-measured-budgets.md)
